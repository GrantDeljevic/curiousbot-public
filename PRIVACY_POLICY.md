# Curiousbot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Curiousbot ("Curiousbot," "the bot," "we," or "us") handles information when it is used in a Discord server. Curiousbot provides community-management and other server features for participating Discord communities.

## Information We Collect

Depending on the features enabled in a server, Curiousbot may process:

- Discord account and server information, such as account names, user IDs, membership, and roles;
- information that users voluntarily submit through forms, registrations, or other bot workflows;
- message content and attachments that are necessary for supported bot features; and
- public profile or similar information requested through a supported workflow.

Curiousbot does not collect online-status or activity information through the Presence intent and does not maintain a general archive of Discord message history. Curiousbot does not use Discord data to train machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models.

## How We Use Information

We use information to provide, operate, maintain, and improve Curiousbot's features, including community-management workflows, registrations, rosters, role management, membership reconciliation, requested public-profile lookups, troubleshooting, and opted-in operational reminders.

Users choose whether to participate in applicable registration and reminder workflows. Information is used for the feature or workflow for which it was submitted or requested.

## Storage and Service Providers

Information may be processed or stored by third-party services that support Curiousbot's operation. When a user requests a lookup using a public profile or similar link, Curiousbot may send the relevant request to the applicable public website or service. Those services have their own terms and privacy policies.

Curiousbot does not retain raw webhook messages or general Discord message history as an archive.

## Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant features and operate participating communities.

Information associated with a retained participant is removed when that participant leaves the server. Users may also request deletion by contacting the developer `.grant` directly on Discord (user ID `282599041244594176`) or by contacting management staff through the relevant server's support channels. Users may request to opt out of an applicable registration or reminder workflow through the same contacts.

## Sharing and Disclosure

Curiousbot does not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Information may be shared with service providers that support the bot, with public services when a user requests a lookup, when a user directs it as part of a workflow, or when required by law.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Third-Party Services

Curiousbot operates through Discord and may rely on other third-party services. Those services are independent of Curiousbot and are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Curiousbot is not responsible for the independent practices of those services.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Curiousbot's features or information practices change. The current version will be made publicly available at the privacy-policy link listed in the Discord Developer Portal. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.

## Contact

For privacy questions, deletion requests, opt-out requests, or security concerns, contact the developer `.grant` directly on Discord (user ID `282599041244594176`) or contact management staff in the relevant participating server's support channels.
